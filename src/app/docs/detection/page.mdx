## Evaluation convention

For mAP@[.50:.95], AP is averaged over the ten IoU thresholds 0.50, 0.55, 0.60, 0.65, 0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85, 0.90, and 0.95. Matching, interpolation, and handling of crowd regions differ between the PASCAL VOC and COCO protocols. EvalSuite will state its convention in every result and will not describe itself as COCO-compatible unless it is validated against the reference COCO evaluation.
